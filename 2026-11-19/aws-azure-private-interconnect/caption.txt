AWS announced general availability of private AWS–Azure connectivity through Interconnect – multicloud on 17 November.

Here’s what changes—and what you still need to design:

• What: A managed private connection between AWS and Azure, outside the public internet.
• Why: Support cross-cloud applications, data movement and phased migrations with less connectivity assembly.
• How: Pair the providers using an activation key, attach your cloud networks and configure routes.
• Security: Provider-link MACsec protects the interconnect. Keep application TLS and least-privilege access.
• Risks: Overlapping IP ranges, broken DNS, route mistakes, latency and unexpected costs.
• Safeguards: Plan addressing, test name resolution, monitor both ends and rehearse recovery.

What would you validate first: routing, DNS or failover?
